Documented Enhancement Proposals (DEPs)
---------------------------------------

Documented Enhancement Proposals (DEPs) are used to propose and document major changes to the pyfar project.
They are intended to provide a structured, transparent, and persistent way to discuss and evaluate proposed changes.
